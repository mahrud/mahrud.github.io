Last time I wrote for this blog was 10 months ago. Not that I didn't want to. Not that I didn't put any time in writing or that I didn't know what to write. I've written a lot this year. I've written about the fate of Iranian people, including the lives lost. I've written about loss, mental health, systematic racism, abolition, mathematicians' unethical role in militarism, surveillance, climate change, prison labor, privileged conversations on the rights, freedoms, and even humanity of minorities, within mathematics or otherwise. I've also written a poem or two, and a draft of an expository paper that serves as the midpoint of my PhD program. Pages upon pages, none finished.

I've struggled with forming a full scale picture of the issues that I preceive. Like an Escher painting that keeps morphing the more you stare at it, problems in our communities, at every scale, keep one-upping each other.
