**tl;dr**: the Minneapolis City Council will soon vote on a proposed [ordinance](https://lims.minneapolismn.gov/Download/File/4860/Facial%20Recognition%20Ordinance%2001.21.2021.pdf) on banning acquisition and use of facial recognition technology by the city. If this matters to you, consider signing [this petition](https://petition.postme.mn/) or joining a [community feedback session](https://postme.mn/index.php/community-feedback-session/) via Zoom tonight (1/26) at 6pm CST. The petition and session are endorsed by groups such as ACLU and EFF, and RT4MN.

If you'd like more background on this issue, the NYTimes article on [Clearview AI](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/01/18/technology/clearview-privacy-facial-recognition.html) and [its operation](https://postme.mn/index.php/clearview-ai/) in Hennepin County and after the [Capitol attack](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/01/09/technology/facial-recognition-clearview-capitol.html) are good places to start, as well as [this article](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/12/29/technology/facial-recognition-misidentify-jail.html) on the third known Black man to be wrongfully arrested based on FR. For a more technical perspective, this is a good [report by the EFF](https://www.eff.org/pages/face-recognition).

Lastly, please consider forwarding this email to others in the Minneapolis/Saint Paul area who may share similar concerns. Facial recognition is one of the few areas where mathematicians have had a hand in social injustices, and I hope we can have a role in fixing those injustices as well.
