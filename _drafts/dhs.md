- (DHS overreaching in what the universities can accomplish more accurately and less intrusively)
  Beyond the absurdity of an agency set up in the aftermath of 9/11 attempting to scrutinize studying in the US out of concern about a “potential for increased risk to national security,” one has to wonder if their concerns hold any water. In the proposal, the DHS mentions only a handful of cases of visa fraud and intellectual property theft possibly on behalf of the Chinese government, while ignoring the futility of the proposed rules in preventing these cases, particularly since China is not in either of the restricted lists. International students are not a national security risk, and we must be clear that such a statement has no justification.
