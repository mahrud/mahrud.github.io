  - Guiding principles
    - available to all (no discrimination)
	- equal opportunity (publish based on work, not author)
	- fair & reliable peer review
	- easy to discover & permanently available
	- transparency of editorial and management
  - fundamental paradox: universities pay researchers to do the work, but publishers get paid for it
  - publishers have to stop online disemination instead of work on printed disemination
  
  - Alternatives:
    - Embargo periods
	- The arXiv model (in math & physics) (note: update your papers)
	- Gold Open Access: free to read, authors pay to publish -> conflict of interest
	- Diamond OA: free to publish, free to read (e.g. Advances in Combinatorics) -> when publishing, consider this!
	  - https://www.cimpa.info/en/node/62
	- Predatory publishers
	  - https://predatoryjournals.com/journals/
