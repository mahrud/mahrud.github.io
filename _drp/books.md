This is a list of some books used by DRP participants in previous semesters.
You are welcome to flip through these books in the library for inspiration,
or choose topics not represented here (for instance, logic or set theory,
category theory, mathematics of gerrymandering or climate change, etc.) and
help us expand it.

### Algebra:
- Visual Group Theory, Carter
- The Symmetric Group, Sagan
- Abstract Algebra, Dummit and Foote

### Number Theory:
- $p$-adic Numbers, Gouvea
- Breaking Codes: An Introduction to Cryptography, Garrett
- Rational Points on Elliptic Curves, Silverman and Tate
- Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves, Silverman

### Analysis:
- Introductory Functional Analysis with Applications, Kreyszig
- Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos, Strogatz

### Applied Math:
- Partial Differential Equations, Evans
- Deep Learning, Goodfellow
- Markov Chains, Norris

### Topology and Geometry:
- Algebraic Topology, Hatcher
- Introduction to Smooth Manifolds, Lee
- Foundations of Differential Manifolds and Lie Groups, Warner

### Graph Theory:
- Introduction to Graph Theory, Trudeau
- Algebraic Graph Theory, Biggs

### Representation Theory:
- Complex Representations of GL$[2,\mathbb{k}]$ for Finite Fields $\mathbb{k}$, Piatetski-Shapiro

More book suggestions are available on the [DRP Network's webpage](https://www.drp-network.org/book-suggestions.html).
Some pairs have also opted to use online notes or various references.
