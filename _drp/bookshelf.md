Thanks to a generous seed grant from the [DRP Network](https://sites.google.com/view/drp-network/),
we have purchased the following 22 books across a variety of topics. The books are
kept at the [Math Library](https://www.lib.umn.edu/math) and are available for those
looking for ideas to browse on top of the reference bookshelf. DRP participants can
borrow these for the entire duration of their participation.

<br />

Interested in donating books to the DRP Bookshelf? Please get in touch! ❤️

### Algebra:
- Groups and Symmetry, Armstrong
- An Introduction to Mathematical Cryptography, Hoffstein Pipher Silverman
- The Symmetric Group, Sagan
- Algebra, Lang

### Number Theory:

- Rational Points on Elliptic Curves, Silverman, Tate
- Number Theory in Function Fields, Rosen

### Analysis:

### Applied Math:
