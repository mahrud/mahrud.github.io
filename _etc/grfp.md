<h2 id="name-of-the-game-demonstrated-potential-for-significant-research-achievements">Name of the game: <br /><center>*Demonstrated Potential for Significant Research Achievements*</center></h2>
<p>… and contributions to their fields throughout their careers.</p>

<p>assessing an applicant’s interests and competencies with consideration given to:</p>
<ul>
  <li>experiences, attributes, and academic achievements and,</li>
  <li>how the applicant has DPSRA</li>
</ul>

<p>Holistic plan for graduate education that takes into account individual interests and competencies:</p>
<ul>
  <li>describes the experiences, attributes, and academic achievements that show how the applicant has DPSRA</li>
  <li>provides a detailed profile of their relevant education, research experience, and plans for graduate education that DPSRA</li>
</ul>

<ul>
  <li>why they want to do it,</li>
  <li>how they plan to do it,</li>

  <li>what benefits could accrue if the project is successful.</li>

<ul>
  <li>the technical aspects of the proposal and</li>
  <li>the way in which the project may make broader contributions.</li>

<p>To that end, reviewers will be asked to evaluate all proposals against two criteria <strong>based on a holistic analysis of the complete application</strong>:</p>

<h1 id="intellectual-merit">Intellectual Merit:</h1>
<p>The Intellectual Merit criterion encompasses <strong>the potential to advance knowledge</strong>.</p>

<p>Intellectual Merit Criterion:</p>
<ul>
  <li>the potential of the applicant to advance knowledge,</li>
  <li>strength of the academic record,</li>
  <li>description of previous research experience or publication/presentations, and references.</li>
</ul>

<p>Evidence:</p>
<ul>
  <li>grades, curricula, awards, etc.</li>
  <li>ability to:
    <ul>
      <li>plan and conduct research</li>
      <li>work as a member of a team or independently</li>
      <li>interpret and communicate research</li>
      <li>take initiative, solve problems, persist</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>

<p><strong>the potential of <em>your appriach</em> to your field of study and your research plan to advance knowledge</strong></p>

<h1 id="broader-impacts">Broader Impacts:</h1>
<p>The Broader Impacts criterion encompasses <strong>the potential to benefit society and contribute to the achievement of specific, desired societal outcomes</strong>.</p>

<p><strong>you</strong> or <strong>your research</strong></p>

<p>Broader impacts may be accomplished:</p>
<ul>
  <li>through the research itself,</li>
  <li>through the activities that are directly related to specific research projects,</li>
  <li>through activities that are supported by, but are complementary to, the project.</li>
</ul>

<p>Broader Impacts Criterion:</p>
<ul>
  <li>the potential of the applicant to benefit society and</li>
  <li>contribute to the achievement of specific, desired societal outcomes,</li>
  <li>including by personal experiences, professional experiences, educational experiences and future plans.</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="synergistic-activities">Synergistic Activities</h3>

<p>Examples that demonstrate the broader impact of the individual’s professional and scholarly activities that focuses on the integration and transfer of knowledge as well as its creation. Examples could include, among others:</p>
<ul>
  <li>innovations in teaching and training (e.g., development of curricular materials and pedagogical methods);</li>
  <li>contributions to the science of learning;</li>
  <li>development and/or refinement of research tools;</li>
  <li>computation methodologies and algorithms for problem-solving;</li>
  <li>development of databases to support research and education;</li>
  <li>broadening the participation of groups underrepresented in STEM; and</li>
  <li>service to the scientific and engineering community outside of the individual’s immediate organization.</li>
</ul>

<h1 id="overall">Overall:</h1>

<p>The following elements should be considered in the review for both criteria:</p>
<ul>
  <li>What is the potential for the proposed activity to:
    <ul>
      <li>Advance knowledge and understanding within its own field or across different fields (Intellectual Merit); and</li>
      <li>Benefit society or advance desired societal outcomes (Broader Impacts)?</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>To what extent do the proposed activities suggest and explore creative, original, or potentially transformative concepts?</li>
  <li>Is the plan for carrying out the proposed activities well-reasoned, well-organized, and based on a sound rationale? Does the plan incorporate a mechanism to assess success?</li>
  <li>How well qualified is the individual, team, or organization to conduct the proposed activities?</li>
  <li>Are there adequate resources available to the PI (either at the home organization or through collaborations) to carry out the proposed activities?</li>
</ul>

<p>NSF values the advancement of scientific knowledge and activities that contribute to achievement of societally relevant outcomes:</p>
<ul>
  <li>full participation of women, persons with disabilities, and underrepresented minorities in STEM;</li>
  <li>improved STEM education and educator development at any level;</li>
  <li>increased public scientific literacy and public engagement with science and technology;</li>
  <li>improved well-being of individuals in society;</li>
  <li>development of a diverse, globally competitive STEM workforce;</li>
  <li>increased partnerships between academia, industry, and others;</li>
  <li>improved national security;</li>
  <li>increased economic competitiveness of the US; and</li>
  <li>enhanced infrastructure for research and education.</li>
</ul>

<p>including the educational and research record, leadership, outreach, service activities, and future plans, as well as individual competencies, experiences, and other attributes.
The aim is to recruit and retain a diverse cohort of early-career individuals with high potential for future achievements, contributions, and broader impacts in STEM and STEM education.</p>
