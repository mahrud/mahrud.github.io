General tutorials and resources:
* [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/) is an open source software for editing SVG files
* [Using Inkscape to create laser cutter files](https://www.scu.edu/media/school-of-engineering/pdfs/maker-lab-resources/Inkscape-for-Laser-Cutter.pdf)
* [CorelDraw tutorial for laser cutting](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JpHI1ab5cOqjf6Y1PttlIn-0-GRAPRQvMxiKsySMY34/edit#heading=h.da58murdnecz)
* [Convenient collection](https://github.com/christianp/aperiodic-monotile) of outlines in various formats.

UMN-specific resources if you'd like to make your own:
* PLS6.75 Laser Cutter at the [Health Sciences Libraries Makerspace](https://libguides.umn.edu/lasercutter)
* PLS6.150D Laser Cutter at [Mechanical Engineering, Room 2-134](https://cse.umn.edu/andersonlabs/pls6150d-laser-cutter)
* [FABstore at Rapson Hall 127](https://design.umn.edu/students/facilities-maker-spaces/fabrication-shops/fabrication-shops-machines-materials) for buying materials
