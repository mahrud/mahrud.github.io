## What is this?

This is an informal postdoc gathering to share our skills and expertise for using Macaulay2 in research.
The aims of this meeting are twofold:
- exchange specific examples of using M2 in forming, proving, or disproving conjectures.
- explore M2 roadblocks and bottlenecks that obstruct our research and discuss solutions.

The ["-fu"](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/-fu) suffix is used to form nouns indicating mastery of specified skill or area of knowledge.

### M2fu is about research, not just programming!

We discuss research questions, M2 computations that led to them, and if there is a missing link, together we dive in to find the algorithmic problems and brainstorm ideas from different perspectives.
(e.g. we might start with a question about $F$-regularity and look into colon ideals or push forwards.)

### Potlucks are about community; everyone contributes!

Everyone uses M2 differently, depending on the unique computational aspects of their research area, therefore everyone is encouraged to pose a problem, example, or technique and open up a discussion.

### Collective doesn't mean a class, seminar, or a Q&A session!

Ultimately, the goal is to form a community of colleagues amongst whom collaborations can be born. We will communicate clearly whether a project is open to collaborators, or an ongoing project obstructed by computational questions which motivate a separate project.
